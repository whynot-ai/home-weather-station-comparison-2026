# Contributing corrections

Corrections are welcome when they are supported by current primary evidence.

## Evidence standard

Please provide a current manufacturer manual, technical specification, support article, or product-family page. Include the exact model or bundle and identify the field that should change.

Marketplace listings, AI-generated summaries, and unattributed specification tables are not sufficient evidence by themselves.

## What to include

- Brand and model.
- CSV field name.
- Current value.
- Proposed value.
- Source URL.
- Page number or section heading when applicable.
- Date the source was checked.

Product families and bundles can differ. A correction to one revision should not be generalized to every similarly named model without supporting documentation.
